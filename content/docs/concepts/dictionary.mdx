---
title: Dictionary
description: Plain definitions of the general terms this documentation uses.
---

These are general terms, borrowed from software practice, that appear in this
documentation. They are not Vatras's own concepts — those are defined in
[Core Terms](/docs/concepts/core-terms).

A term belongs here if it appears in these pages and a reader might reasonably
not know it. General programming vocabulary that Vatras never uses does not.

### Ambiguity

When a word could mean more than one thing, or when two words are being used
for the same thing without anyone having decided which is right.

### Architecture Decision Record

A short written record of a decision that would be costly to reverse: what was
chosen, and why. Numbered so it can be referred to later. Often shortened to
ADR, or called a decision record.

### Bounded context

From domain-driven design: a part of a system with its own consistent
language, where a word means one specific thing. The same word can mean
something else elsewhere — an "order" in billing is not an "order" in
shipping.

### Canonical

The agreed version, where several exist. A canonical term is the one the
project uses; the alternatives are recorded as ones to avoid.

### Cardinality

How many of something there can be. "A project has exactly one vocabulary" and
"decisions accumulate" are both statements about cardinality.

### Domain

The subject matter a piece of software is about — banking, logistics,
publishing — as distinct from the technology used to build it.

### Domain model

A structured understanding of a domain: its concepts, what they mean, and how
they relate. Vatras uses [Project Model](/docs/project-model) instead, because
what it captures is broader than the domain alone.

### Glossary

A list of terms with their definitions. Vatras uses
[vocabulary](/docs/context/vocabulary) for the shared language itself, and
treats a glossary as one way of writing it down.

### Ubiquitous language

From domain-driven design: a shared set of terms used consistently by everyone
working on a project — in conversation, in documentation and in the code — so
that one concept is not called three different things. This is what Vatras
calls a project's [vocabulary](/docs/context/vocabulary).
