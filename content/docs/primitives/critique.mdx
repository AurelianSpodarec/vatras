Challenge an understanding to find what is weak, assumed, missing, or
otherwise worth questioning.

## What it does

Critique takes something you currently believe or intend and tries to
break it.

It looks for:

- assumptions that have not been established
- weaknesses or contradictions
- missing considerations
- alternative explanations or approaches

Critique does not try to solve every problem it finds. It makes the
problems visible so they can be investigated or the understanding can be
changed.

## Example

Given:

```text
We should use the old mine to get behind the orcs.
```

Critique might find:

```text
The plan assumes the mine is accessible.
The orcs may already know about the entrance.
The mine may not actually provide a route behind their position.
```

Those are findings to work with, not a replacement plan.

## Input

An idea, proposal, understanding, decision, or other piece of work.

## Output

Objections, assumptions, weaknesses, alternatives, and questions worth
investigating.
