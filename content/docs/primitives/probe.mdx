Explore an understanding by asking targeted questions and following the
answers.

## What it does

Probe is interactive. It does not ask a fixed list of questions.

Each answer can expose a new uncertainty, change the next question, or
resolve part of the understanding.

The aim is to reach the important unknowns rather than to ask questions
for their own sake.

## Example

```text
Why does one global vocabulary work?

→ Because terms should mean the same thing everywhere.

What happens when two contexts need different meanings?

→ Then vocabulary may need to be scoped per context.
```

The next question follows from the previous answer.

## Input

An understanding with unresolved questions, assumptions, or decisions.

## Output

Resolved questions, clarified assumptions, and a better-understood
model.
