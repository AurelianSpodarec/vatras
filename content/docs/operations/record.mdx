Record makes durable project context reflect what has been learned or
changed during the work.

It is not simply a command to write something down. Record determines
whether something from the work should survive the current interaction
and, if so, makes the appropriate context reflect it.

## What it does

Record reviews the work and asks what, if anything, should become durable.

```text
Work
  ↓
What changed or was learned?
  ↓
Should it survive this interaction?
  ↓
Where does it belong?
  ↓
Create, update, or reconcile context
```

If nothing needs to become durable, Record does nothing.

## What can be recorded

The destination depends on what was learned.

For example:

- a new project term belongs in [**Vocabulary**](/docs/context/vocabulary)
- an important decision belongs in the project's decision record
- a useful finding may belong in research
- a change to the project's understanding may belong in the project model

Record does not require everything to become the same kind of document.
The knowledge determines the appropriate destination.

## Record can be used independently

Recording does not depend on another flow.

It can follow different kinds of work:

```text
Grill
  ↓
Record

Sharpen
  ↓
Record

Build
  ↓
Record

Verify
  ↓
Record
```

It can also be invoked on its own to review existing context against the
current understanding.

```text
Existing context
    ↓
Review
    ↓
Find missing, stale, or contradictory knowledge
    ↓
Reconcile context
```

## What Record does not do

Record does not preserve everything that happened.

Temporary conversation, intermediate reasoning, and information with no
value beyond the current interaction do not need to become project
context.

Record also does not mean that every finding must be written somewhere.
Sometimes the correct result is that nothing needs to change.

## Relationship to flows

A flow may use Record when its result should become durable context.

For example:

```text
Grill
  ↓
Record
```

This is a composition of the [**Grill**](/docs/flows/grill) flow and the
Record operation. Recording is therefore not inherently part of Grill.

## Skill

`vatras:record`

The skill is the agent-facing interface for invoking Record.
